New Zealand Wildlife

About New Zealand and our place in space

New Zealand has many unique animals because it had very few mammals before humans arrived. This allowed birds like the kiwi, weka and kākāpō to develop. The moa was another flightless bird, but it is now extinct. NZ is also home to lizards, frogs, bats and many butterflies. Some of these animals are rare or endangered, so protecting them is important.

Because Aotearoa has been cut off from other landmasses for so long, our islands have developed a unique ecosystem of animals. Before the introduction of pest species, birds were the dominant class of species in New Zealand. We only had three native mammals on land, and all were bats.
