About New Zealand and our place in space

Introduction

Aotearoa New Zealand is a country in the pacific ocean, southeast of Australia. For this hackathon, we interpreted the theme of "Our place in space" as the space being Aotearoa, and we chose to use a website to talk about our place in this island we call home.

This website is intended to be used for people to learn about New Zealand and its history, and to help the users understand more about our place in the space of Aotearoa

There are 67,000 kiwis in New Zealand. The Kiwi is Aotearoa's national bird, and is a symbol of our country both overseas and in our space
